03 · Expansion language

Three layout directions

The same feature set can feel compact, balanced, or expressive. The center option is the recommended baseline.

A · Dense stackFast
Control Center420 × 404

Best for smaller displays. Three controls per row; lowest pointer travel, but labels have less breathing room.

B · Balanced cardsRecommended
Control Center590 × 510

Clear two-column rhythm, readable device states, and a natural width for launcher and clipboard content.

C · Command deckExpressive

Asymmetric and rich. Excellent on ultrawide displays, but visually heavier and less consistent across utilities.
